---
title: "Security"
description: "How to report a vulnerability."
---

Please report vulnerabilities privately, not in a public issue:

- Use GitHub's **private vulnerability reporting** on the [graphowl/graphowl](https://github.com/graphowl/graphowl/security) repository, or
- email <connect@graphowl.app> with the subject `SECURITY`.

We acknowledge reports within five working days. See the [security policy](https://github.com/graphowl/graphowl/blob/main/SECURITY.md) and our [`security.txt`](https://graphowl.app/.well-known/security.txt).
